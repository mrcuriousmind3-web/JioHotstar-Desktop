Get started

Up and running in minutes

Three simple steps to get started.

1
Download the right file

Open the latest release and choose the installer under Assets for your system.

2
Install the app

Windows: open the .exe. Linux: open the .deb in your software installer and select Install.

3
Launch and sign in

Open JioHotstar Desktop and sign in on the official website if required.
